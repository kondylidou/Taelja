Axiom 1: nato(sweden)
Axiom 2: nato(germany)
Axiom 3: attacked(liechtenstein,germany)
Axiom 4: nato(X) /\ nato(Y) /\ attacked(Z,Y) => protects(X,Y)

Goal 1: protects(sweden,germany)
Proof:
  have nato(sweden)
    by axiom 1
   and nato(germany)
    by axiom 2
   and attacked(liechtenstein,germany)
    by axiom 3
  hence protects(sweden,germany)
    by axiom 4
