Axiom 1: q(a,a)
Axiom 2: q(X,Y) => p(f(X))
Axiom 3: p(X) => q(X,Z)

Lemma 4: q(X,Y) => q(f(X),Z)
Proof:
  have q(X,Y)
    by assumption
  hence p(f(X))
    by axiom 2
  hence q(f(X),Z)
    by axiom 3

Goal 1: q(f(f(f(a))),f(f(f(a))))
Proof:
  have q(a,a)
    by axiom 1
  hence q(f(a),X)
    by lemma 4
  hence q(f(f(a)),Y)
    by lemma 4
  hence q(f(f(f(a))),f(f(f(a))))
    by lemma 4
