Axiom 1: catches(james,mouse)
Axiom 2: cat(james)
Axiom 3: catches(james,X) => brings(james,X)
Axiom 4: brings(X,mouse) /\ cat(X) => happy(X)

Goal 1: happy(james)
Proof:
  have catches(james,mouse)
    by axiom 1
  hence brings(james,mouse)
    by axiom 3
   and cat(james)
    by axiom 2
  hence happy(james)
    by axiom 4
