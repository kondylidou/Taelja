Axiom 1: product(a,b,c)
Axiom 2: product(X,identity,X)
Axiom 3: product(X,X,identity)
Axiom 4: product(X,Y,Z) /\ product(Y,A,B) /\ product(X,B,C) => product(Z,A,C)
Axiom 5: product(identity,X,X)
Axiom 6: product(X,Y,Z) /\ product(Y,A,B) /\ product(Z,A,C) => product(X,B,C)

Goal 1: product(b,a,c)
Proof:
  have product(c,identity,c)
    by axiom 2
   and product(a,a,identity)
    by axiom 3
   and product(c,a,b)
    by axioms
  hence product(b,a,c)
    by axiom 4
