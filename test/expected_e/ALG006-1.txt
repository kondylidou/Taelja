Lemma 5: difference(difference(X,Y),X) = difference(X,X)
Proof:
  difference(difference(X,Y),X)
= { by axiom 1 }
  difference(difference(X,X),difference(Y,X))

  difference(difference(X,difference(Y,X)),X)
