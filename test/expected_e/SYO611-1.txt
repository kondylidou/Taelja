Axiom 1: le(X,X)
Axiom 2: le(max(X,Y),Z) => le(X,Z)
Axiom 3: le(max(X,Y),Z) => le(Y,Z)
Axiom 4: eq(f(X),a0)

Goal 1: eq(f(X),a0)
Proof:
  have eq(f(X),a0)
    by axiom 4

Goal 2: eq(f(max(max(X,s(Y)),Z)),a0)
Proof:
  have eq(f(max(max(X,s(Y)),Z)),a0)
    by axiom 4

Goal 3: le(s(X),max(max(Y,s(X)),Z))
Proof:
  have le(max(max(Y,s(X)),Z),max(max(Y,s(X)),Z))
    by axiom 1
  hence le(max(Y,s(X)),max(max(Y,s(X)),Z))
    by axiom 2
  hence le(s(X),max(max(Y,s(X)),Z))
    by axiom 3
