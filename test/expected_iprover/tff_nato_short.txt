Axiom 1: nato(sweden)
Axiom 2: nato(germany)
Axiom 3: attacked(liechtenstein,germany)
Axiom 4: attacked(X,Y) /\ nato(Y) /\ nato(Z) => protects(Z,Y)

Goal 1: protects(sweden,germany)
Proof:
  have attacked(liechtenstein,germany)
    by axiom 3
   and nato(germany)
    by axiom 2
   and nato(sweden)
    by axiom 1
  hence protects(sweden,germany)
    by axiom 4
