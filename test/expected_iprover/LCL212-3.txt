Axiom 1: or(not(X),Y) = implies(X,Y)
Axiom 2: axiom(implies(X,or(Y,X)))
Axiom 3: axiom(X) => theorem(X)
Axiom 4: theorem(implies(X,Y)) /\ theorem(X) => theorem(Y)

Lemma 5: theorem(implies(X,implies(Y,X)))
Proof:
  have axiom(implies(X,or(not(Y),X)))
    by axiom 2
  hence theorem(implies(X,or(not(Y),X)))
    by axiom 3
  hence theorem(implies(X,implies(Y,X)))
    by rw axiom 1

Goal 1: theorem(implies(not(p),implies(q,implies(implies(p,q),q))))
Proof:
  have theorem(implies(implies(q,implies(implies(p,q),q)),implies(not(p),implies(q,implies(implies(p,q),q)))))
    by lemma 5
   and theorem(implies(q,implies(implies(p,q),q)))
    by lemma 5
  hence theorem(implies(not(p),implies(q,implies(implies(p,q),q))))
    by axiom 4
