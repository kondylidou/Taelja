Axiom 1: subgroup_member(a)
Axiom 2: subgroup_member(b)
Axiom 3: product(a,inverse(b),c)
Axiom 4: subgroup_member(X) /\ subgroup_member(Y) /\ product(X,inverse(Y),Z) => subgroup_member(Z)

Goal 1: subgroup_member(c)
Proof:
  have subgroup_member(a)
    by axiom 1
   and subgroup_member(b)
    by axiom 2
   and product(a,inverse(b),c)
    by axiom 3
  hence subgroup_member(c)
    by axiom 4
