Axiom 1: less_equal(divide(X,Y),X)
Axiom 2: less_equal(zero,Z)
Axiom 3: less_equal(divide(divide(Z,A),divide(B,A)),divide(divide(Z,B),A))
Axiom 4: divide(zero,Z) = zero
Axiom 5: less_equal(Z,B) => divide(Z,B) = zero
Axiom 6: less_equal(Z,B) /\ less_equal(B,Z) => Z = B
Axiom 7: divide(Z,B) = zero => less_equal(Z,B)

Lemma 8: divide(divide(a,zero),a) = zero
Proof:
  have less_equal(divide(a,zero),a)
    by axiom 1
  hence divide(divide(a,zero),a) = zero
    by axiom 5

Lemma 9: less_equal(divide(divide(a,zero),a),divide(divide(a,a),zero))
Proof:
  have less_equal(zero,divide(divide(a,a),zero))
    by axiom 2
  hence less_equal(divide(divide(a,zero),a),divide(divide(a,a),zero))
    by rw lemma 8 R->L

Lemma 10: divide(divide(a,a),zero) = zero
Proof:
  have less_equal(divide(divide(a,a),divide(zero,a)),divide(divide(a,zero),a))
    by axiom 3
  hence less_equal(divide(divide(a,a),zero),divide(divide(a,zero),a))
    by rw axiom 4
   and less_equal(divide(divide(a,zero),a),divide(divide(a,a),zero))
    by lemma 9
  hence divide(divide(a,a),zero) = divide(divide(a,zero),a)
    by axiom 6
  hence divide(divide(a,a),zero) = zero
    by rw lemma 8

Lemma 11: less_equal(divide(divide(a,a),zero),divide(divide(a,zero),divide(a,zero)))
Proof:
  have less_equal(zero,divide(divide(a,zero),divide(a,zero)))
    by axiom 2
  hence less_equal(divide(divide(a,a),zero),divide(divide(a,zero),divide(a,zero)))
    by rw lemma 10 R->L

Lemma 12: divide(divide(a,zero),divide(a,zero)) = zero
Proof:
  have less_equal(divide(divide(a,zero),divide(a,zero)),divide(divide(a,a),zero))
    by axiom 3
   and less_equal(divide(divide(a,a),zero),divide(divide(a,zero),divide(a,zero)))
    by lemma 11
  hence divide(divide(a,zero),divide(a,zero)) = divide(divide(a,a),zero)
    by axiom 6
  hence divide(divide(a,zero),divide(a,zero)) = zero
    by rw lemma 10

Lemma 13: less_equal(divide(divide(a,zero),divide(a,zero)),divide(divide(a,divide(a,zero)),zero))
Proof:
  have less_equal(zero,divide(divide(a,divide(a,zero)),zero))
    by axiom 2
  hence less_equal(divide(divide(a,zero),divide(a,zero)),divide(divide(a,divide(a,zero)),zero))
    by rw lemma 12 R->L

Goal 1: divide(a,zero) = a
Proof:
  have less_equal(divide(divide(a,divide(a,zero)),divide(zero,divide(a,zero))),divide(divide(a,zero),divide(a,zero)))
    by axiom 3
  hence less_equal(divide(divide(a,divide(a,zero)),zero),divide(divide(a,zero),divide(a,zero)))
    by rw axiom 4
   and less_equal(divide(divide(a,zero),divide(a,zero)),divide(divide(a,divide(a,zero)),zero))
    by lemma 13
  hence divide(divide(a,divide(a,zero)),zero) = divide(divide(a,zero),divide(a,zero))
    by axiom 6
  hence divide(divide(a,divide(a,zero)),zero) = zero
    by rw lemma 12
  hence less_equal(divide(a,divide(a,zero)),zero)
    by axiom 7
   and less_equal(zero,divide(a,divide(a,zero)))
    by axiom 2
  hence divide(a,divide(a,zero)) = zero
    by axiom 6
  hence less_equal(a,divide(a,zero))
    by axiom 7
   and less_equal(divide(a,zero),a)
    by axiom 1
  hence divide(a,zero) = a
    by axiom 6
