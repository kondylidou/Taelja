Axiom 1: ocean(indian)
Axiom 2: borders(indian,northern_territory)
Axiom 3: borders(X,Y) => borders(Y,X)
Axiom 4: borders(indian,western_australia)
Axiom 5: borders(western_australia,northern_territory)
Axiom 6: state(northern_territory)
Axiom 7: state(western_australia)

Goal 1: state(western_australia)
Proof:
  have state(western_australia)
    by axiom 7

Goal 2: state(northern_territory)
Proof:
  have state(northern_territory)
    by axiom 6

Goal 3: borders(western_australia,northern_territory)
Proof:
  have borders(western_australia,northern_territory)
    by axiom 5

Goal 4: borders(western_australia,indian)
Proof:
  have borders(indian,western_australia)
    by axiom 4
  hence borders(western_australia,indian)
    by axiom 3

Goal 5: borders(northern_territory,indian)
Proof:
  have borders(indian,northern_territory)
    by axiom 2
  hence borders(northern_territory,indian)
    by axiom 3

Goal 6: ocean(indian)
Proof:
  have ocean(indian)
    by axiom 1
