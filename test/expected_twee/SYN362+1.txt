Goal 1: big_r(X,w(X)) /\ (big_r(z,Y) /\ big_p(Y) => $false) /\ big_p(Y) => $false
Proof:
  have big_r(z,w(z))
    by assumption 1
   and big_p(w(z))
    by assumption 3
  hence $false
    by assumption 2
  hence big_r(X,w(X)) /\ (big_r(z,Y) /\ big_p(Y) => $false) /\ big_p(Y) => $false
    by discharge
