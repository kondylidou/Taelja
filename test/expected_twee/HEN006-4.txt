Axiom 1: less_equal(divide(X,Y),X)
Axiom 2: less_equal(zero,X)
Axiom 3: divide(zero,X) = zero
Axiom 4: less_equal(divide(divide(Z,A),divide(B,A)),divide(divide(Z,B),A))
Axiom 5: divide(X,X) = zero
Axiom 6: less_equal(X,Y) /\ less_equal(Y,X) => X = Y
Axiom 7: divide(X,Y) = zero => less_equal(X,Y)
Axiom 8: less_equal(X,Y) => divide(X,Y) = zero
Axiom 9: less_equal(divide(a,b),d)

Lemma 10: divide(X,zero) = X
Proof:
  have less_equal(zero,divide(divide(X,divide(X,zero)),divide(zero,divide(X,zero))))
    by axiom 2
  hence less_equal(divide(divide(X,zero),divide(X,zero)),divide(divide(X,divide(X,zero)),divide(zero,divide(X,zero))))
    by rw axiom 5 R->L
   and less_equal(divide(divide(X,divide(X,zero)),divide(zero,divide(X,zero))),divide(divide(X,zero),divide(X,zero)))
    by axiom 4
  hence divide(divide(X,zero),divide(X,zero)) = divide(divide(X,divide(X,zero)),divide(zero,divide(X,zero)))
    by axiom 6
  hence zero = divide(divide(X,divide(X,zero)),divide(zero,divide(X,zero)))
    by rw axiom 5
  hence zero = divide(divide(X,divide(X,zero)),zero)
    by rw axiom 3
  hence less_equal(divide(X,divide(X,zero)),zero)
    by axiom 7
   and less_equal(zero,divide(X,divide(X,zero)))
    by axiom 2
  hence divide(X,divide(X,zero)) = zero
    by axiom 6
  hence less_equal(X,divide(X,zero))
    by axiom 7
   and less_equal(divide(X,zero),X)
    by axiom 1
  hence divide(X,zero) = X
    by axiom 6

Lemma 11: divide(divide(a,b),d) = zero
Proof:
  have less_equal(divide(a,b),d)
    by axiom 9
  hence divide(divide(a,b),d) = zero
    by axiom 8

Lemma 12: less_equal(divide(divide(a,d),divide(b,d)),zero)
Proof:
  have less_equal(divide(divide(a,d),divide(b,d)),divide(divide(a,b),d))
    by axiom 4
  hence less_equal(divide(divide(a,d),divide(b,d)),zero)
    by rw lemma 11

Lemma 13: divide(divide(b,d),b) = zero
Proof:
  have less_equal(divide(b,d),b)
    by axiom 1
  hence divide(divide(b,d),b) = zero
    by axiom 8

Lemma 14: zero = divide(divide(a,d),divide(b,d))
Proof:
  have less_equal(zero,divide(divide(a,d),divide(b,d)))
    by axiom 2
   and less_equal(divide(divide(a,d),divide(b,d)),zero)
    by lemma 12
  hence zero = divide(divide(a,d),divide(b,d))
    by axiom 6

Goal 1: less_equal(divide(a,d),b)
Proof:
  have less_equal(divide(divide(divide(a,d),b),divide(divide(b,d),b)),divide(divide(divide(a,d),divide(b,d)),b))
    by axiom 4
  hence divide(divide(divide(divide(a,d),b),divide(divide(b,d),b)),divide(divide(divide(a,d),divide(b,d)),b)) = zero
    by axiom 8
  hence divide(divide(divide(divide(a,d),b),divide(divide(b,d),b)),divide(zero,b)) = zero
    by rw lemma 14 R->L
  hence divide(divide(divide(divide(a,d),b),divide(divide(b,d),b)),zero) = zero
    by rw axiom 3
  hence divide(divide(divide(a,d),b),divide(divide(b,d),b)) = zero
    by rw lemma 10
  hence divide(divide(divide(a,d),b),zero) = zero
    by rw lemma 13
  hence divide(divide(a,d),b) = zero
    by rw lemma 10
  hence less_equal(divide(a,d),b)
    by axiom 7
